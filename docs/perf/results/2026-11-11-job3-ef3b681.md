## Setup

- Commit `ef3b681` (`origin/claude/0.10-faster-turns`: round 3 accepted, M14). `package-lock.json` is unchanged since the baseline, so no `npm ci`.
- Mac worker (M2, 8 cores, 16 GB, mains power), Node v24.10.0, npm 11.6.0, 2026-11-11, about 01:28–01:31 CET. The whole check ran in one chain under `one-at-a-time --min-free-gb 2`.
- **Load:** the average was 2.27 when the check started (below 3, as M14 asks) and 2.79 after the head starts. The 13.9 and 10 that the later steps show are the check's own load (vitest with 8 workers and the parallel tournaments); other apps (Chrome, the window server) were as usual.
- Bench timings are the seconds the bench prints (one decimal), 3 runs each after the verification run as warm-up. `npm test` 3 times after one warm-up.
