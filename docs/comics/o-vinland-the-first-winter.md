# The First Winter

**Logline:** The supply ship from Greenland never came before the ice. Now a small Norse settlement in Vinland is running out of food and iron, and its stubborn headman insists they need nobody. A cautious trade of red cloth for meat and furs opens a door. When two children, one from each people, are carried out to sea on a drifting ice floe, the two peoples have to work as one to bring them home.

**Cover moment:** Ragna offers a bolt of red cloth and a small iron knife to Tashek, who has arrived with a sled of furs and dried meat, while Ketil stands behind her and the two children, Sveinn and Pemi, watch each other from behind the adults. It is the splash on **page 3** (panel 4).

**Setting:** A Norse settlement of three turf houses on a terrace above a frozen bay on the northern coast of Newfoundland, in the deep winter of a year around 1000.

**Cast**
- **Ragna:** a Norse settler in her thirties, Ketil's wife. A long blue cloak with a fur collar and a round bronze brooch, a red dress, a white headscarf over fair hair. Clear-headed and generous; she keeps the stores and knows exactly how little is left.
- **Ketil:** the settlement's smith, about forty. A grey knitted cap, a brown beard, a grey fur-trimmed cloak over a blue tunic, a broad belt with a pouch. Has a forge and almost no iron left to work.
- **Sveinn:** their son, nine. Fair hair, a brown cloak with fur trim. Bored, brave and badly in need of a friend.
- **Ozur Stiff-Neck:** headman of the settlement, sixty. A big grey beard, a bearskin over his shoulders. Proud: "Norsemen manage alone."
- **Tashek:** a hunter of the people who had long lived on these shores (ancestors, perhaps, of the Beothuk or the Innu). Black hair to his shoulders, a long caribou-skin parka with thick fur trim and a band of stitched pattern at the hem, skin boots. Careful, observant, a quiet joker.
- **Wenaya:** Tashek's wife. A long black braid, a caribou-skin parka with patterned bands. Knows the ice better than anyone, and can read Norse faces surprisingly well.
- **Pemi:** their daughter, eight. A fur-trimmed caribou-skin hood and parka. Fearless, quick, very good at snowball fights.

*Tashek's family speak their own language, which the Norse do not understand. Their lines are given in English in angled brackets, < >. The names are invented for the story.*

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing panel. Three long turf houses with grass roofs, half buried in snow, on a terrace above a frozen bay. Thin smoke rises straight up from their roof holes into a pale winter sky. On the shore, a longship rests under a snow-covered timber shelter. Dark spruce forest and white hills behind.
CAPTION: Vinland. The deep of winter, around the year one thousand.
CAPTION: The ship from Greenland should have come in the autumn, with grain and iron and news. The ice came first.

**Panel 2.** Interior of the main house: a long central hearth, benches along the walls, a loom, hanging pots. Ragna kneels by a row of storage barrels, looking into one. It's nearly empty.
RAGNA: Barley, a handful. Dried fish, two weeks. Cheese, one, and Sveinn has been at it.
SVEINN (from a bench, mouth full): ...Mmf. Not much.

**Panel 3.** Ketil at his cold forge in a corner, holding up a tiny lump of rough bog iron, not much bigger than a hen's egg.
KETIL: And iron? This is the whole of what I got from the bog this autumn. Enough for one knife. Maybe.

**Panel 4.** Ozur Stiff-Neck sits in the high seat, bearskin over his shoulders, arms folded.
OZUR: Then we tighten our belts. Norsemen manage alone. We always have.
RAGNA: Ozur, we'll tighten our belts until they go round twice.

**Panel 5.** Sveinn at the door, peering out through a crack, eyes wide.
SVEINN: Mother! Somebody's coming over the ice!

## PAGE 2

**Panel 1.** Outside. Across the white bay, small figures walk towards the settlement: two adults and a child, pulling a long wooden sled piled with furs.
CAPTION: Across the frozen bay came a sled.

**Panel 2.** At the doorway, a crowd of Norse settlers in fur hoods. Ozur pushes to the front, scowling.
OZUR: Stay inside. We don't know them.
RAGNA: We've seen their smoke all winter, Ozur, in the hills. We've just never been brave enough to say hello.

**Panel 3.** Tashek stops at a respectful distance at the foot of the terrace. He raises an empty hand in greeting. Wenaya stands behind him, a hand on Pemi's shoulder.
TASHEK: < We saw that your smoke is getting thinner. People with thin smoke are often hungry. >
WENAYA: < Say it more slowly. They look frightened. >
TASHEK: < Slowly won't help. They don't speak *any* of it. >

**Panel 4.** Ragna looks at Ketil. Ketil looks at the little lump of iron in his hand, then at Ragna, and nods.
RAGNA: We have so little to offer.
KETIL: We have the red cloth. And I can finish the knife.

**Panel 5.** A small panel. Ketil hammers at the forge, sparks flying; the tiny knife takes shape.
SFX: TINK! TINK! TINK!
CAPTION: He worked through the night with the last of the charcoal.

## PAGE 4

**Panel 1.** Tashek takes the knife, turns it over, and tests the edge on a sliver of wood. He nods, impressed, but Wenaya shows him the fine stone blade at her own belt and shrugs.
WENAYA: < Ours cuts too. But the cloth... the cloth is new. >
TASHEK: < Then we'll take both and be polite about the knife. >

**Panel 2.** Tashek and Wenaya unload half of their sled onto the snow: caribou furs, bundles of dried meat, a skin bag of fat. Ragna's eyes widen.
RAGNA: That's too much! That's more than a fair trade!
KETIL: I don't think they're trading, Ragna. I think they're *helping*.

**Panel 3.** Meanwhile, the two children have drifted together behind the adults. Pemi scoops a handful of snow, packs it, and throws it right into Sveinn's face.
SFX: PAFF!

**Panel 4.** Sveinn, face white with snow, shocked. Then he grins and throws one back. Pemi dodges, laughing.
SVEINN: Oh, that's *war*!
SFX: PAFF! PAFF! PAFF!

**Panel 5.** The four adults turn to look. Within seconds two small faces are covered in snow and both children are laughing too hard to stand up. Tashek and Ketil look at each other and, for the first time, both laugh too.
TASHEK: < Children don't need anyone to translate. >
KETIL: I think she's winning.

## PAGE 5

**Panel 1.** Tashek points across the bay towards a frozen inland lake beyond the shore ridge, mimes cutting a hole and pulling up a fish on a line.
TASHEK: < Fish. Under the ice. Plenty. Come. >
RAGNA: He's saying there's fish. Under the *ice*?

**Panel 2.** Back at the doorway, Ozur, arms folded.
OZUR: Norsemen fish from boats. Not from holes like a seal.
RAGNA: Then stay here and eat your pride, Ozur. I'll bring you some fish to go with it.

**Panel 3.** On the frozen lake. Wenaya cuts a round hole in the ice with a sharpened bone pick, chipping fast. Ragna kneels beside her, trying it with Ketil's new knife, which immediately gets stuck.
SFX: chik chik chik chik
RAGNA: Your way is better.
WENAYA: < Yes. >

**Panel 4.** Tashek drops a line with a bone hook through the hole and jiggles it gently. Ketil kneels beside him, absolutely fascinated.
KETIL: No net. No boat. Just patience and a hole.
TASHEK: < And a fish who is curious. Like you. >

**Panel 5.** Tashek hauls up a fat trout, flapping in the cold air. Ketil cheers like a boy.
SFX: FLAP FLAP!
KETIL: HA!

## PAGE 7

**Panel 1.** The adults come over the shore ridge from the lake with their fish. Wenaya sees first and drops her catch.
WENAYA: < PEMI! >
RAGNA: SVEINN!

**Panel 2.** At the settlement. Ozur bursts out of the door at the shouting, bearskin flying. He sees the floe, the black water, the two small figures.
OZUR: By all the... the wind's taking them out to sea!

**Panel 3.** Ketil runs towards the longship's shelter.
KETIL: The ship's boat! We'll row out!
TASHEK (grabbing his arm, shaking his head, pointing): < No! The ice at the edge will break under a heavy boat. You'll fall in before you even reach the water. >

**Panel 4.** Wenaya kneels and draws fast in the snow with her finger: the shore, the drifting floe, an arrow curving round to the rocky point at the east of the bay.
WENAYA: < When the tide turns, the ice always swings to the point. There. That's where we can reach them. >
RAGNA (looking at the drawing): She's saying the floe will drift to the point!

**Panel 5.** Ozur, for once, doesn't argue.
OZUR: Then we go to the point. What do we need?
KETIL: Rope. A boat that doesn't break the ice. And something that does.

## PAGE 8

**Panel 1.** A busy panel full of people. Norse settlers drag the long walrus-hide rope from the longship's rigging. Tashek and Ketil lash the light ship's boat onto Tashek's long sled to spread its weight. Ozur pushes from behind.
CAPTION: Everyone ran. Everyone carried something.
OZUR: PUSH! Push, by thunder!

**Panel 2.** Out on the drifting floe. The children huddle together. Pemi shows Sveinn how to lie flat on the ice to spread their weight. She wraps the red cloth around both of them.
PEMI: < Lie down. Flat like a fish. Ice likes flat. >
SVEINN: You're telling me to lie down? All right. I'm lying down.

**Panel 3.** On the rocky point, the rescue party lines up. In the grey dusk, against the white ice, the red cloth on the floe shines like a little flame.
TASHEK: < There! The red! I can see them! >
RAGNA: The red cloth! We can see it!

**Panel 4.** The floe swings in on the tide towards the point, exactly as Wenaya said. But a strip of black water and broken ice still lies between.
KETIL: Close. Not close enough.
WENAYA: < Close enough for the boat. >

**Panel 5.** Ketil and Tashek push the boat-sled to the edge of the firm ice. Tashek tests the edge with his bone ice pick, tapping, listening.
SFX: tok... tok... tuk.
TASHEK: < Here, strong. Here, no. Launch HERE. >

## PAGE 10

**Panel 1.** That night. Inside the Norse long house, crowded and warm. The central hearth blazes. Tashek's family sit on the benches with the settlers, the children wrapped in furs by the fire, steaming gently.
CAPTION: That night, the long house was full.

## PAGE 12

**Panel 1.** Ragna stands at the barrels, now holding dried fish, dried meat, a bag of fat. Not full, but enough.
RAGNA: We'll last until the ship comes. And if it doesn't come, we'll last anyway.

**Panel 2.** Sveinn on the headland shouting, pointing out to sea.
SVEINN: SAIL! A SAIL! THE SHIP FROM GREENLAND!

**Panel 3.** Far out at sea, a tiny square sail with red stripes, crossing the broken ice towards the bay.
CAPTION: The ship from Greenland, half a year late.

**Panel 4.** On the shore, Ozur and Tashek watch it come in, side by side, arms folded the same way.
OZUR: They'll bring grain and iron. And they'll ask us how we lasted the winter.
TASHEK: < What will you tell them? >
OZUR (as if he understood): I'll tell them we had neighbours.

**Panel 5.** FINAL PANEL, wide. The white hillside above the bay, the turf houses smoking behind. Two sleds race down the slope side by side, throwing up snow: Sveinn in new caribou-skin mittens, Pemi with the red cloth flying out behind her like a banner, the little iron fish-hook glinting on her parka. Ketil, Ragna, Tashek and Wenaya stand at the top, laughing. Far out on the bay, the striped sail of the ship from Greenland comes home.
CAPTION: The first winter was over. The settlement still stood. And it no longer stood alone.
