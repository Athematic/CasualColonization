# The Explorer's Map

**Logline.** Captain Bartholomew Quayle has spent his last silver on an old map that shows a city of golden towers up a river on an island the charts get wrong, and his rival Captain Lucretia Marsh has bought a copy too. The race ashore ends in the hands of Wakaya, a Kalinago canoe captain who knows exactly what the map shows and finds the whole thing very funny. By the end, young navigator Toby Wren has learned that the best map is one drawn by two people who have both been there.

**Cover moment.** Captain Quayle leaning over the parchment map in the great cabin of the *Swallow*, one finger on the coast, while Toby holds the telescope beside him and both look out through the stern windows at the green island, its mountain, its river mouth and the *Golden Gull* sailing alongside. It is the large panel at the top of **page 2** (panel 1).

**Setting.** An island of the Lesser Antilles in the Caribbean, home of the Kalinago, with a tall green volcanic peak, rainforest and a river mouth on its leeward coast. Spring, 1721.

## Cast

- **Captain Bartholomew Quayle**, master of the sloop-of-trade *Swallow*. Fifties, dark beard, long dark blue coat with gold trim and buttons, gold-edged tricorn, lace neck-cloth. Big-hearted, romantic, terrible with money.
- **Toby Wren**, his young navigator, fifteen. Tousled light-brown hair, red waistcoat, white shirt with rolled sleeves, brown breeches, a brass telescope he never puts down. Draws maps beautifully and is just beginning to suspect that maps can be wrong.
- **Captain Lucretia Marsh**, master of the *Golden Gull*, which flies a plain yellow pennant. Forties, sharp, green coat, a hat with a long curling plume. Quayle's rival for twenty years and, though neither admits it, his oldest friend.
- **Wakaya**, a Kalinago canoe captain and fisherman. Forties, strong, hair cut in a straight fringe and long behind, a cotton band below each knee and on his upper arms, a cotton loincloth, red body paint of roucou on his shoulders. Knows every reef, current and river of the island. Laughs easily, mostly at other people's maps.
- **Ioulou**, his daughter, about fourteen. Sharp-eyed, quick, wears a cotton wrap and shell necklace. Would very much like to try Toby's telescope.

## PAGE 1

**Panel 1.** Wide opening panel. Dawn on a sparkling blue sea. The *Swallow*, a trim two-masted trading vessel, cuts through the waves under full sail. High up on the mainmast, a small figure stands in the crow's nest with a telescope.

CAPTION: The Caribbean Sea, spring of 1721.

**Panel 2.** Close on Toby in the crow's nest, telescope to his eye, mouth open wide.

TOBY: LAND HO! LAND HOOOO!

**Panel 3.** The door of the stern cabin bursts open on deck. Captain Quayle charges out in his nightshirt, tricorn and one boot, waving a rolled parchment.

QUAYLE: Where away, Toby? WHERE AWAY?

**Panel 4.** From the crow's nest, Toby points with the telescope. Ahead: a tall green volcanic peak rising from the sea, wrapped in a ring of white cloud.

TOBY: Dead ahead, Captain! A great green mountain! Just like on the map!

**Panel 5.** Toby swings the telescope to one side and freezes.

TOBY: And... oh no. Sail to starboard. Yellow pennant.

**Panel 6.** Small panel. Quayle, on deck, closes his eyes in pain.

QUAYLE: Marsh.

## PAGE 2

**Panel 1.** Large panel, top half of the page: THE COVER MOMENT. The great cabin in the stern of the *Swallow*: warm brown panelling, a hanging brass lantern, a big wooden chart table. On it, a hand-drawn parchment map of an island coast with a compass rose, little pictures of ships, a whale and two sea serpents (no words or letters), brass dividers, a compass, a quill and inkpot, an hourglass. Quayle, now in his blue coat, leans over the map, one finger on the coast. Toby, telescope in hand, stands beside him. Both look out through the square stern windows at the bright green island with its mountain and river mouth, and the *Golden Gull* sailing alongside.

**Panel 2.** Close on the map, Quayle's finger following the river inland to a small drawing of a city with tall golden towers at the foot of the mountain.

QUAYLE: There. Up the river, under the mountain. The Golden City. An old sailor in a harbour tavern drew it with his own hand. He saw it from the sea, at sunset, shining.

**Panel 3.** Toby, doubtful, picking up the dividers and measuring.

TOBY: Captain, how much did the old sailor charge for it?

QUAYLE: Everything I had. Worth every coin.

**Panel 4.** Toby looks out of the window at the *Golden Gull*, where a figure in a plumed hat on the quarterdeck is studying an identical map.

TOBY: Then how did Captain Marsh get one?

QUAYLE: (darkly) He must have drawn two.

## PAGE 3

**Panel 1.** Wide. The river-mouth bay. Two longboats race towards the beach: the *Swallow's* boat with Quayle standing in the bow, and the *Golden Gull's* with Captain Marsh, plume streaming, beating time for her rowers.

MARSH: Put your backs into it, lads! Second place gets nothing but sand!

QUAYLE: PULL! Pull for glory! Pull for gold!

**Panel 2.** Toby, sitting in the stern of Quayle's boat, has his telescope turned the other way, towards a big dugout canoe with a small square sail, anchored near the reef. Two people in it are fishing and watching the race with interest.

**Panel 3.** Close on the canoe: Wakaya, with a fishing line, and Ioulou, leaning on the gunwale. Ioulou is laughing.

IOULOU: Papa, they are rowing straight for the rocks.

WAKAYA: Both of them. It's like watching two turtles race to the cooking pot.

**Panel 4.** Both longboats hit the shallow reef just short of the beach at the same moment and stop dead. Captains and crews tumble forward in heaps.

SFX: KRRUNCH! / KRRUNCH!

**Panel 5.** Quayle and Marsh, knee-deep in the warm water, hats askew, glaring at each other.

MARSH: Quayle.

QUAYLE: Marsh.

MARSH: You're in my way.

QUAYLE: You're in my lagoon.

---

## PAGE 4

**Panel 1.** The beach. The two crews wade ashore. Wakaya's canoe glides through a gap in the reef, which neither longboat found, and slides up onto the sand. Wakaya steps out, coiling his line. Ioulou follows.

WAKAYA: (in French) Good morning. You came in very fast. Most people use the door. (points at the gap in the reef)

**Panel 2.** Toby steps forward eagerly, answering in French.

TOBY: Good morning! You speak French!

WAKAYA: Some French. Some Spanish. A little English. Traders come here from everywhere. They all ask the same question.

CAPTION: Toby's French is not good. Wakaya's is better.

**Panel 3.** Quayle unrolls his map on the sand. Marsh unrolls hers beside it, a little further along the beach, pretending not to look at his.

QUAYLE: Sir! We seek the Golden City. Up this river, under the mountain. Do you know it?

**Panel 4.** Wakaya crouches and studies the map closely, then looks at Ioulou. They both burst out laughing.

SFX: HA HA HA!

**Panel 5.** Wakaya, wiping his eyes.

WAKAYA: Yes. I know it. Two days up the river. Bring good shoes.

QUAYLE: Then it's real?

WAKAYA: It is very real. You can't miss it. Many people have tried.

---

## PAGE 5

**Panel 1.** Captain Marsh strides up, rolling her map with a snap.

MARSH: Two days? Nonsense. We'll do it in one. We don't need a guide who laughs at us. Gulls! Follow the river!

**Panel 2.** Wide. Marsh's party marches off into the forest at a brisk pace, carrying shovels, empty sacks and a large chest. Marsh's plume bobs at the front.

WAKAYA: (calling after them) Not that way! That's the river that goes up the mountain! It is hot up there!

MARSH (off): Hot is good! Gold likes hot!

**Panel 3.** Quayle hesitates, looking from Marsh's disappearing party to Wakaya. Toby tugs his sleeve.

TOBY: Captain. He knows the island. The map doesn't even know where the reef is.

**Panel 4.** Quayle takes off his tricorn and bows to Wakaya.

QUAYLE: Then I'd be honoured if you would guide us, sir. What would you take in payment?

**Panel 5.** Wakaya considers this.

WAKAYA: Fishhooks. Good iron ones. Twelve.

QUAYLE: Twelve fishhooks for the Golden City? Done!

IOULOU: (aside, to Toby) Papa always asks for fishhooks. He says gold is no good for fishing.

---

## PAGE 6

**Panel 1.** Wide jungle panel. The party follows a narrow trail through rainforest: giant tree ferns, buttress roots, hanging lianas, bright flowers, a hummingbird. Wakaya leads, barefoot and easy; Ioulou behind; Quayle, sweating in his heavy coat; Toby, sketching in a notebook; two sailors carrying a small cask.

CAPTION: Up the river.

**Panel 2.** Wakaya stops at a fork in the trail and kneels by the river. He dips a hand in.

WAKAYA: Feel. This branch is cold. It comes from the high springs. That branch is warm. It comes from the mountain's belly. Your friend went that way.

TOBY: (dipping his hand, astonished) It's like bath water!

**Panel 3.** Rest stop on a mossy rock. Toby shows Wakaya his sketch of the coast; Wakaya takes a stick and draws in the damp earth, fast and sure.

WAKAYA: Your map has the coast like this. (draws a smooth line) The coast is really like this. (draws bays, points, reefs) Here the current runs north in the morning. Here, sharks sleep. Here, the water is so shallow a crab can walk to the next island.

**Panel 4.** Close on Toby, staring at the drawing in the earth, then at his parchment map, then back.

TOBY: Captain. Everything on our map is a little bit wrong.

QUAYLE: (cheerfully, mopping his brow) But the city is on it, my boy. That's the part that matters.

**Panel 5.** Ioulou, behind Quayle's back, grinning to Toby. Toby grins back despite himself.

---

## PAGE 7

**Panel 1.** Night. A camp by the river, hammocks of woven cotton strung between trees (Wakaya brought them; the sailors look delighted). A small fire. Wakaya hands round flat rounds of cassava bread and grilled fish.

SAILOR: A hanging bed! Captain, we must get these for the ship!

WAKAYA: You can. For more fishhooks.

**Panel 2.** Quayle, in a hammock, rocking happily, hands folded on his stomach, tells a story with expansive gestures. Ioulou listens with a critical face.

QUAYLE: ...and the sea serpent was as long as three ships, with eyes like lanterns!

IOULOU: Did it have the little curly tail like on your map?

QUAYLE: Exactly like that!

IOULOU: Then it was a pelican. They look like that when they are wet.

**Panel 3.** Toby and Wakaya sit by the fire. Toby has his telescope; Wakaya is weaving a fishing line.

TOBY: Why didn't you tell us what the city is?

WAKAYA: Because you would not believe me. Some things you only believe with your own eyes. That's why people travel.

**Panel 4.** A distant, faint shout echoes from the mountain above. Everyone sits up.

FAR-OFF VOICE: HALLOOOOO! ...ANYBODY! ...HELLOOOO!

**Panel 5.** Close on Quayle, swinging out of the hammock and landing in a heap, but already pulling on his coat.

QUAYLE: That's Marsh.

## PAGE 8

**Panel 1.** Early dawn, high on the mountainside. Wide. A barren, steaming valley of yellow and white rock, with bubbling hot pools and vents of steam, the colours flat and strange. On a narrow ledge above a steaming pool, Marsh and her crew are huddled together, the large chest beside them. The ledge has crumbled away at both ends.

MARSH: We followed the warm river. It got warmer.

**Panel 2.** Wakaya surveys the scene calmly from the edge of the valley, hands on hips.

WAKAYA: I said it was hot.

MARSH: (weakly) You did say that.

**Panel 3.** Wakaya and Ioulou cut long, tough lianas from the forest edge and twist them into a rope with practised speed. Toby and Quayle help, getting tangled.

QUAYLE: Toby, I appear to be tied to a tree.

TOBY: That's your end, Captain. Hold on!

**Panel 4.** Ioulou runs out along a safe rock spine and throws the vine rope. Marsh catches it.

SFX: FWIPP!

**Panel 5.** One by one, the *Gull's* crew swing across to safety, hand over hand. Marsh comes last. Quayle reaches out and hauls her the final yard.

QUAYLE: Lucretia.

MARSH: Bartholomew. Not a word.

QUAYLE: I wasn't going to say anything.

**Panel 6.** Behind them, the abandoned treasure chest teeters on the ledge and slides gently into the steaming pool.

SFX: blorp.

MARSH: That was my good chest.

## PAGE 9

**Panel 1.** Wide. The combined party, now one group, follows Wakaya along the cold river through the forest. Marsh walks next to Quayle, plume drooping with steam, sharing his water.

CAPTION: The second day.

**Panel 2.** Late afternoon. The trail climbs to a ridge. Wakaya stops and holds aside a curtain of vines, looking back at the others with a little smile.

WAKAYA: Here it is. Your Golden City.

**Panel 3.** Big panel, two thirds of the page width, the most beautiful panel in the story. From the ridge they look down into a deep gorge above the sea. Rising from it, tall, straight, many-sided pillars of pale grey rock stand in clusters like towers and walls, some taller than church spires. The low sun strikes them and they glow golden against the dark green forest and the blue sea beyond.

**Panel 4.** The whole party stares, silent. Toby lowers his telescope slowly.

TOBY: Towers. Hundreds of towers.

**Panel 5.** Close on Quayle's face, wonder turning to understanding.

QUAYLE: It's stone. It's just stone. Shining.

---

## PAGE 10

**Panel 1.** Wakaya sits on the ridge, legs dangling, entirely satisfied. Ioulou sits beside him.

WAKAYA: The mountain made them, long before my grandmother's grandmother. When the fire-rock cooled, it cracked into posts. From the sea at sunset, every sailor sees a city. We call it the place where the sun builds houses.

**Panel 2.** Marsh sits down heavily on a rock and takes off her hat.

MARSH: Twenty years chasing rumours, and the best one was a pile of rocks.

**Panel 3.** But Toby has turned his telescope beyond the pillars, down to the sea. His face lights up.

TOBY: Captain. Captain Marsh. Look below the towers.

**Panel 4.** Through the telescope (circular frame): a deep, round, sheltered bay, closed off from the open sea by a curve of reef with one clear gap, a waterfall pouring fresh water into it, a white beach, calm water. A perfect harbour.

**Panel 5.** Wakaya nods, pleased that someone has finally looked at the right thing.

WAKAYA: That is the real treasure, if you want one. When the great storms come in the late summer, every canoe on this coast runs into that bay. The water is still. The river is sweet. Nothing on your map, eh?

**Panel 6.** Quayle and Marsh look at each other. Slowly, both captains start to laugh.

QUAYLE: A safe harbour, fresh water and a pilot who knows the reef.

MARSH: For a trading captain, that is worth more than a city of gold.

---

## PAGE 11

**Panel 1.** Back on board the *Swallow*, evening, the great cabin as on the cover, lantern lit. The old map is pushed aside. Toby and Wakaya bend over a fresh sheet of parchment, drawing together. Ioulou peers through Toby's telescope out of the stern window, delighted.

CAPTION: That evening.

**Panel 2.** Close on the new map as it takes shape, all in little pictures (no words): the true coastline with its bays and points; the reef drawn as a row of tiny rocks with the gap marked by a small canoe; arrows for the currents; the warm river drawn with little curls of steam; the safe bay with a tiny waterfall; and the stone towers drawn honestly as rocks, with a small sun above them.

WAKAYA: Here you put a turtle. Turtles lay eggs on that beach. Nobody touches them in the egg season.

TOBY: A turtle. Got it.

**Panel 3.** Quayle and Marsh at the cabin table with Wakaya, now with cups of chocolate. A small pile of iron fishhooks, needles and knives sits beside a bundle of cotton hammocks and a basket of cassava.

QUAYLE: Twelve fishhooks, as agreed. And more, if you'll trade with us. Both our ships.

MARSH: (grudgingly) Both our ships. Together. Just this once.

**Panel 4.** Wakaya taps the new map.

WAKAYA: Then we are friends. Friends use the bay in the storm season. Friends ask before they cut our trees. Friends bring fishhooks.

QUAYLE: Friends. Agreed.

**Panel 5.** Close on Toby, looking from the old map to the new one, thoughtful.

TOBY: The old sailor's map was beautiful. But this one is true.

---

## PAGE 12

**Panel 1.** Morning. Wide. Both ships, the *Swallow* and the *Golden Gull*, lie at anchor in the sheltered bay below the stone towers, the waterfall spilling into the lagoon. Wakaya's canoe, sail up, leads a second ship's boat through the reef gap.

CAPTION: The next morning, in the bay below the towers.

**Panel 2.** On the *Swallow's* quarterdeck, Quayle hangs the new map in a frame on the cabin wall, as if it were a family portrait. Toby watches proudly.

QUAYLE: Best map I've ever owned. And I didn't pay a penny for it.

TOBY: You paid twelve fishhooks, Captain.

QUAYLE: The best twelve fishhooks I ever spent.

**Panel 3.** Marsh leans over from the boat alongside, holding out her own copy of the old tavern map.

MARSH: Bartholomew. What do we do with these?

QUAYLE: Keep them. Frame them. Hang them next to the true one. A good reminder never to buy a city in a tavern.

**Panel 4.** Ioulou has climbed to the *Swallow's* crow's nest with Toby's telescope. Toby, below on the ratlines, calls up to her.

TOBY: What can you see?

**Panel 5.** Final panel, large and wide, the bottom half of the page. From high behind Ioulou in the crow's nest: the whole leeward coast of the island laid out below in bright morning sun, the green mountain with its ring of cloud, the golden stone towers in their gorge, the bay with its two ships, Wakaya's canoe a tiny shape at the reef gap. Ioulou has the telescope pointed straight down at the beach, where her own village of oval thatched houses stands among the palms, smoke rising, people waving up at the ships.

IOULOU: (big balloon, joyful) LAND HO! It's my house!

CAPTION: THE END.
